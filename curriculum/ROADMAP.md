# Roadmap — From Letters to Fluency

This is the master plan. It maps the course to the international **CEFR** scale (A1 → C2) and to the Russian state exam system **ТРКИ / TORFL**.

> **Honest expectations.** Russian is rated a "hard" language for English speakers (the US Foreign Service Institute estimates about **1,100 hours** of instruction to reach professional working proficiency). The alphabet is a weekend; the grammar (six cases, verb aspects, verbs of motion) is the real mountain. Everything below is an **estimate** — individual pace varies a lot. What matters is **daily consistency**.

## At a glance

| Stage | Level | ТРКИ exam | Approx. study hours | What you can do at the end |
|---|---|---|:---:|---|
| **0 — Alphabet & Sounds** | pre-A1 | — | 10–15 | Read any word aloud; greet, thank, introduce yourself; read signs and menus |
| **1 — Survival Russian** | A1 | ТЭУ (Элементарный) | ~100 | Handle basic daily situations: introduce yourself, order food, ask directions, tell the time |
| **2 — Everyday Russian** | A2 | ТБУ (Базовый) | ~150 | Talk about your past, plans, family, work, shopping, health; understand slow, clear speech |
| **3 — Independent Speaker** | B1 | ТРКИ-1 (Первый) | ~250 | Hold conversations on familiar topics, tell stories, give opinions, read simple texts |
| **4 — Fluent Conversation** | B2 | ТРКИ-2 (Второй) | ~350 | Argue a viewpoint, follow films & news, read real articles, write clearly structured texts |
| **5 — Advanced & Native-like** | C1 (→ C2) | ТРКИ-3 (Третий) | 500+ (ongoing) | Understand literature & nuance, express yourself spontaneously, handle professional contexts |

**Rough time-to-B2 at different paces:**

| Daily study | Time to B2 (≈ 850–950 h) |
|:---:|:---:|
| 1 hour / day | ≈ 2½ years |
| 2 hours / day | ≈ 15 months |
| 3 hours / day | ≈ 10 months |

*Real-world exposure (podcasts, YouTube, conversation) counts — and speeds things up.*

---

## How the course teaches

1. **Grammar just-in-time** — introduced only when you need it for real sentences.
2. **Output early** — every lesson ends with something you say/write yourself (to your tutor in the chat).
3. **Spaced repetition** — every lesson recycles earlier ones; your tutor schedules reviews at +1, +3, +7, +14, +30 days.
4. **Stress marked on every word** (until B1) — Russian stress is unpredictable and hurts comprehension if wrong.
5. **Fading support** — transliteration disappears after Stage 0; English explanations give way to Russian at B1 and are almost gone by B2.
6. **Four skills** — reading, writing, listening, speaking in every unit.
7. **Checkpoints** — each unit has a short check; each stage ends with a test (pass mark **80%**).

Each lesson is **45–60 minutes**. 4–5 lessons per week is a good pace; 1 per day is faster. Units are ~4–6 lessons.

## Stage 1 — Survival Russian *(A1)* · ~12 units

**Goal:** real, if simple, conversations. **Cases covered:** Nominative, Prepositional (location), Accusative (direct object & direction), a first taste of Genitive (*нет, у меня*).

| Unit | Title | Key grammar | Key vocabulary / functions |
|:-:|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Знакомство** — Getting to know people | Gender of nouns · "to be" is absent · *Я из…* · 1st-conjugation verbs (*знать, понимать, читать*) · *Меня зовут* · negation | Countries & nationalities · professions · languages · small talk |
| 2 | **Мой мир** — My things, my people | Plural of nouns · all possessives (*мой, твой, наш, ваш, его, её, их*) · *У меня есть…* | Family · objects in a room · numbers 0–20 |
| 3 | **Что ты делаешь?** — Actions | Present tense: both conjugations (*-ать/-ять*, *-ить/-еть*) · *хотеть, мочь* (irregular) · adverbs | Daily activities · hobbies · *Я люблю + inf./noun* |
| 4 | **Где?** — Location | Prepositional case singular (*в/на + P*) · *в* vs *на* | Places in a city · home · *Где ты живёшь?* |
| 5 | **Куда?** — Direction & transport | Accusative of direction (*в/на + A*) · *идти / ехать* (present) · imperative of *извините, скажите* | Directions (*прямо, налево, направо*) · transport · tickets |
| 6 | **Еда и покупки** — Food & shopping | Accusative singular (inanimate/animate) · *Я хочу…* · *Можно…?* · *Дайте, пожалуйста* | Café & market · numbers 20–100 · prices · *Сколько стоит?* |
| 7 | **Время** — Time | Telling time · days · months · *в + A* for days · *когда?* | Calendar · routines · appointments |
| 8 | **Какой?** — Describing | Adjectives (nominative, m/f/n/pl) · colours · *какой?* | Appearance · weather (*холодно, жарко*) · places |
| 9 | **Семья и возраст** — Family & age | Genitive after *нет* · *У меня нет…* · *Мне … лет* · numerals with nouns | Extended family · age · likes (*Мне нравится…*) |
| 10 | **Мой день** — Routine | Reflexive verbs (*просыпаться, умываться*) · frequency adverbs | A day in your life · meals · habits |
| 11 | **В городе** — In the city | *Мне нужно…* · *Мне нравится…* · set phrases for service situations | Café · shop · hotel · pharmacy · asking for help |
| 12 | **Stage 1 Review & Checkpoint** | — | Mixed skills test: reading · listening · writing · speaking |

## Stage 2 — Everyday Russian *(A2)* · ~13 units

**Goal:** talk about the past and future; use *all six cases* in singular and plural; start verbs of motion and aspect.

| Unit | Title | Key grammar |
|:-:|---|---|
| 13 | **Вчера** — Yesterday | Past tense (*был, была, было, были*) · regular & irregular past forms |
| 14 | **Завтра** — Tomorrow | Future with *буду + infinitive* · plans · intro to perfective future |
| 15 | **Родительный падеж I** — Genitive singular | Possession · absence · quantity (*много, мало, бутылка воды*) · prepositions *от, до, из, с, без, для, у, около, после* |
| 16 | **Родительный II & numerals** | Genitive plural · numerals 1–4 / 5+ with nouns · shopping amounts |
| 17 | **Дательный падеж** — Dative | *мне / тебе / ему* · *нравится, надо, нужно, можно, нельзя* · *по* · age · giving & telling |
| 18 | **Творительный падеж** — Instrumental | *с + I* · professions (*работать врачом*) · *заниматься спортом* · *познакомиться с…* |
| 19 | **Все падежи** — All cases, plural | Plural declension of nouns · adjective declension (all cases) · review table |
| 20 | **Глаголы движения I** — Verbs of motion I | *идти/ходить, ехать/ездить* · prepositions of motion · prefixes preview |
| 21 | **Вид I** — Aspect I | Imperfective vs perfective · when to use which in the past |
| 22 | **Просьбы и приказы** — Requests & commands | Imperative · *давай(те)* · modal verbs (*хотеть, мочь, уметь, должен*) |
| 23 | **Сравнения** — Comparisons | Comparative & superlative · *чем* · *самый* |
| 24 | **Рассказ** — Telling stories | *потому что, если, когда, чтобы, что, потом, после* · simple connectors |
| 25 | **Stage 2 Review & Checkpoint** | A2 test (ТБУ-style) |

## Stage 5 — Advanced & Native-like *(C1 → C2)* · open-ended

**Goal:** near-native comfort; nuance, humour, register and culture.

* **Literature:** Pushkin, Lermontov, Gogol, Tolstoy, Dostoevsky, Chekhov, Bulgakov, Akhmatova, Brodsky — read, annotate, discuss.
* **Style & rhetoric:** functional styles (scientific, official, journalistic, colloquial, literary); advanced syntax.
* **Nuance:** aspect and motion verbs in figurative language; word order for emphasis; intonation and implied meaning.
* **Culture & humour:** wordplay, jokes, anekdoty, regional and generational slang, proverbs.
* **Professional Russian:** your field (law, science, business, technology…).
* **Translation practice** both ways.
* **Exams:** ТРКИ-3 (C1), ТРКИ-4 (C2) practice papers.

## Pace guide

* Never skip the **stage checkpoints**. If you score under 80%, repeat the weak lessons.
* Missed a few days? Don't restart — tell your tutor and do a **short review session** first.
* Plateau at B1? That's normal. Increase listening/reading; add conversation.

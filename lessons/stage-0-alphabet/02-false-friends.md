**Stage 0 · Alphabet** · ⏱ 40–50 min · New letters: **В Н Р С У Х Е**

## 2. The seven false friends

This is the single most important lesson for reading. These letters are traps: your eyes say one thing, the sound is another. Learn them as **"looks like → actually"**.

| Letter | Looks like | Actually sounds like | Memory hook |
|:---:|:---:|---|---|
| **В в** | B | **v** as in *van* | **В**е́ра = **V**era |
| **Н н** | H | **n** as in *net* | **Н**ос = **n**ose |
| **Р р** | P | **r** — a **rolled or tapped r**, like Spanish *pero* | **Р**ома́н = **R**oman |
| **С с** | C | **s** as in *sun* — **never** "k" or "ch" | **С**естра́ = **s**ister |
| **У у** | Y | **oo** as in *boot* | looks like a "Y" — say "**oo**" as in "y**ou**" without the "y" |
| **Х х** | X | **kh** — a breathy, scraping *h* from the back of your throat, like Scottish *lo**ch*** or German *Ba**ch*** | **Х**ор = **kh**or |
| **Е е** | E | **ye** as in ***ye**s* (when stressed) | **Е** + нет → *n**ye**t* |

### About Е
When **Е** is stressed it sounds like **ye** (*нет* = *nyet*). When it is **not** stressed it becomes weaker, like a short **ee**: **сестра́** = *sees-TRA*, **метро́** = *mee-TRO*. You'll learn the full pattern in Lesson 7 — for now just follow the respelling column.

### About Р
You need a rolled/tapped **r** (one quick tap of the tongue tip against the ridge behind your upper teeth). If you can't roll it yet, don't worry — practise saying *"butter"* quickly in American English: the "tt" is nearly the same tap. Use it for **р**.

### About Х
Don't say it like English **h** (*hat*). It's rougher: air scrapes the back of the tongue. Try whispering a long *"kh-h-h"* as if fogging a mirror… but from further back.

> ⚠️ **A spoiler you'll thank me for:** an **unstressed О** is pronounced like **А** ("ah"). That's why **она́** is *a-NA* and **у́тро** is *OO-tra*. Full rules in Lesson 7. Trust the respelling column for now.

## 5. Grammar peek — **вот**

**Вот** is a pointing word: "here/there is…". Use it with nothing else.

| Russian | English |
|---|---|
| **Вот ма́ма.** | Here's mom. |
| **Вот метро́.** | There's the metro. |
| **Вот рестора́н, а там метро́.** | Here's a restaurant, and there's the metro. |
| **Вот А́нна, а там Анто́н.** | Here's Anna, and there's Anton. |
| **Нет, вот Ве́ра.** | No, here's Vera. |

# Lesson 9 — Stage 0 Checkpoint

**Stage 0 · Alphabet** · ⏱ 45–60 min · 🏁 **Final test of the alphabet stage**

## 🎯 Purpose

Prove to yourself that you can **read Russian**. This test covers Lessons 1–8: letters, sounds, sound rules, signs, menus, a short text and the survival phrases.

**How to take it**
- Do it in one sitting, **no peeking** at earlier lessons or the reference sheets.
- Write your answers in a notebook (or tell them to your tutor in the chat).
- Score yourself with the answer key at the end.
- **Pass mark: 80/100.** Below that is *not* failure — the scoring guide tells you exactly what to revisit.

## Part C — Rules (10 points, 1 each)

1. How is **молоко́** really pronounced?
2. How is **друг** really pronounced?
3. How is **вход** really pronounced?
4. How is **что** pronounced?
5. How is **Здра́вствуйте** pronounced?
6. Which three consonant letters are **always hard**?
7. Which three consonant letters are **always soft**?
8. What does **Ь** do?
9. What is **Й** called, and what does it sound like?
10. What is the soft partner of **у**? And of **ы**?

## Part E — Reading (10 points, 2 each)

> Приве́т! Меня́ зову́т Окса́на. Я врач. Э́то моя́ семья́: муж Андре́й, дочь Ка́тя и сын Анто́н. Мы в Москве́. Андре́й — учи́тель. Ка́тя — студе́нтка. Анто́н — ребёнок.

Answer in English:

1. What is her name?
2. What is her job?
3. Who is Andrei, and what is his job?
4. Who are Katya and Anton? (How are they related to Oksana?)
5. Where do they live / where are they?

## 🎉 What you can do now

You can:

- read **any Russian word aloud** (even if you don't understand it yet);
- greet people, thank them, say sorry, introduce yourself and say what you do;
- ask **Где…? Что э́то? Как дела́? Кто э́то?**;
- say **Я не понима́ю** and **Повтори́те, пожа́луйста**;
- read signs, menus and short texts in the real world.

That's the foundation of the whole language. **Everything from here is vocabulary and grammar — and you can already read all of it.**
